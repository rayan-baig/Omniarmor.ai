How we handle your data

Plain words. Every line on this page matches what the app does today.

The short version

  • OmniArmor is a product of MTRX Horizon. "We" on this page means MTRX Horizon, which looks after your data.
  • We keep what you type in, so the app can do its job.
  • We don't sell your data. We don't show ads. We don't use tracking or analytics tools.
  • A few outside companies help run the site. They are all listed below.
  • A workspace owner can delete the whole workspace at any time. (Cancel a paid Academy plan first.)

What we collect

When you sign up: your name, email, password, company name, time zone and the industries you pick. We store your password scrambled (hashed), so nobody can read it, not even us.

When you use the tracker: the dates (and, for checks that count hours or minutes, the times), numbers and yes-or-no answers you enter, any limits you change, and who changed what and when. That last part is the audit trail.

Emails we send: we keep a copy of each email (reminders, time-limit alerts and password reset emails), so you can see it in Radar. Reminders go out once a day. Checks that count hours or minutes also send an alert when they get close to their limit and when they pass it; we look for those every {{ 'minute' if facts.alert_minutes == 1 else facts.alert_minutes ~ ' minutes' }}.

When a kid uses the Academy: a nickname, a picture, their Armo helper, their track, their levels and stars, the days they played, the questions they got wrong (for practice), their friends and their duels. We never ask a kid for a real name, email, birthday or password. Please pick a nickname, not a real name.

When you visit:

  • The server writes a log line for each page you open: your IP address, the time, the page, the page you came from, and your browser type. We hide password reset links, invite links and the link back from a Stripe payment in that log, and we shorten any email address in it to its first letter and its domain (like p***@example.com).
  • We save the date you last used the app (just the date).
  • When someone types a wrong password, asks for a password reset, signs up, or tries a kid join code or a friend code, we note the email typed (if any) and the IP address. This stops people from guessing passwords and codes.

When something breaks: we save an error report with the page address (with the links above hidden) and what went wrong in our code. Our Autopilot emails new problems to the people who run OmniArmor, at most {{ facts.operator_emails_per_hour }} emails an hour; past that, or when email isn't set up, the same message goes to the server log, with email addresses shortened.

When you pay for the Academy plan: we keep Stripe's customer and subscription numbers and your plan's status, renewal date and, if you cancel, the date it ends. We never see your card number.

Cookies

  • We use one cookie. It keeps you signed in and stops other sites from sending fake forms as you. The page's own code can't read it, and it is only sent over a secure connection. It belongs to OmniArmor's own address only: MTRX Horizon's other apps, on their own addresses, never get it. When you sign in (or a kid joins with a code), it lasts {{ facts.session_days }} days from the last time you use the site. Before that, it only holds a form token and goes away when you close your browser.
  • Armo, the helper, keeps two tiny notes in your browser tab: whether it already said hello, and which alert you closed. They go away when you close the tab.
  • No ad cookies. No tracking cookies.

Why we use it

  • To run the tracker: count down deadlines, show what needs attention and send reminders.
  • To keep accounts safe: sign-in, limits on wrong guesses, and error reports so we can fix bugs.
  • To run the Academy and its plan.
  • To count users. We count how many accounts there are and how many were used in the last 30 days. Only those two numbers go to our own admin dashboard. No names, no emails, no companies.

Who else sees it

Inside your workspace:

  • Everyone on your team can see the readings, reports, audit trail, reminder log, and the team list with names and emails.
  • A kid who joins on a device with a join code can only use the Academy.
  • Academy friends, who can be in other families, see a kid's nickname, picture, rank and stars, and duel scores. There is no chat.

The people at MTRX Horizon who run OmniArmor can open the database when they need to fix something. They also get Autopilot's emails about problems: error reports, failing backups, and reminder emails that keep failing (with the error the email service gave).

Outside companies:

  • Our hosting company runs the server. It stores the database, the backups and the server logs.
  • Our email service delivers our emails, so it sees your email address and the message.
  • Google Fonts. Our pages load their fonts from Google. That means Google sees your IP address and browser each time you open a page.
  • Stripe handles Academy payments. When you pay, Stripe gets your email, your card details and your workspace number.

That's the full list. We don't sell your data or share it for ads.

  • Kid join codes work for {{ facts.join_code_days }} days and friend codes for {{ facts.friend_code_days }} days. Then they are deleted.
  • Answers from a quiz nobody finished: {{ facts.quiz_check_days }} days.
  • Error reports: {{ facts.error_days }} days in the app. A copy emailed to the people who run OmniArmor stays in their inbox.

How to delete it

  • The whole workspace: the owner goes to Settings, then Delete workspace, and types the company name. This deletes the company, every reading, the audit trail, the reminder log, every kid's Academy profile and every team account that isn't in another workspace. Download your report and audit trail first if you need them.
  • If you pay for the Academy plan, cancel it first on the Academy plan page (Manage or cancel). Deleting is blocked until you do, so you aren't charged for a workspace that's gone. Once it's cancelled you can delete right away, even before the paid time runs out.
  • One team member: the owner removes them in Settings. Their account is deleted too, unless they belong to another workspace. There is no button to delete only your own account, so ask your workspace owner. Changes you made stay in that workspace's audit trail and report, with your name, because that record is what an inspector checks.
  • One kid: a grown-up opens the Academy, then Manage learners, then Remove. Their progress, friends and duels go too.
  • A kid's devices: a grown-up can sign a kid out on every device from the kid's map page.
  • Reminder emails (time-limit alerts too): turn them off for yourself in Settings.

Deleted means gone from the app right away. Copies can still sit in backups, server logs and Stripe for the times listed above.

What we promise and what we don't

We promise:

  • Passwords are stored scrambled.
  • Card numbers never reach us.
  • No ads, no tracking tools, and we don't sell your data.

We don't promise:

  • That nothing will ever go wrong. We work hard to keep your data safe, but no website is perfectly safe.
