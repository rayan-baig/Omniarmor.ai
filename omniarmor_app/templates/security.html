Security and your data

What a school, a parent or a business owner usually asks first, answered plainly. The Privacy page has every detail.

Where your data lives

  • Everything you type into OmniArmor is kept in one database on one server, run for {{ COMPANY }} by our hosting company. {{ COMPANY }} makes OmniArmor and looks after that data.
  • Every page is sent over a secure connection (HTTPS), and browsers are told to use only the secure address. The sign-in cookie is sent only over that connection, and the page's own code can't read it.
  • Passwords are stored scrambled (hashed), so nobody can read them, not even us. Card numbers go straight to Stripe and never reach us.
  • Our pages, fonts and pictures all come from our own server. No ad, analytics or tracking company sees when you or a student opens a page.
  • {{ ASSISTANT }}, the optional AI helper, runs in your browser on your own AI key and talks to the AI company you chose. Our server never sees the key or the chat, and {{ ASSISTANT }} is never on a page a student uses.

Who can see a student's name

  • A teacher adds students by first name or nickname only. We never ask for a student's last name, email, birthdate or photo, and students have no accounts or passwords.
  • The names can be seen by the grown-ups signed in to that school's workspace: the teacher, and any colleague the school invites. Nobody in another school or another workspace can.
  • A student who joins with a code sees only their own lessons. If the teacher turns the class leaderboard on (it starts off), classmates see each other's first name or nickname and lessons done, for that class only.
  • The one-page trial report for the school office shows totals only, never a name.
  • {{ ASSISTANT }} never reads the class list. A name goes to the teacher's AI company only if the teacher types it to add students.
  • The people at {{ COMPANY }} who run OmniArmor can open the database when they need to fix something. They don't use it for anything else, and we never sell or share it for ads.

How to delete it

  • One student: on the class page, the teacher removes them. Their progress goes with them, at once.
  • A whole class: the teacher deletes the class on its page. Its students and their progress go too.
  • One kid in a family: a grown-up opens the Academy, then Manage learners, then Remove.
  • Everything: the workspace owner goes to Settings, then Delete workspace, and types the name. Every record, the audit trail, the reminder log, every class and every learner are deleted. (Paying for a plan? Cancel it first on the Plans page; you can delete right after.) Download the report and History first if you need them.
  • Deleted means gone from the app right away. Copies can still sit in backups for the time above, and in the server logs for as long as our hosting company keeps them.

Questions, or something looks wrong

Write to us at {{ facts.support_email }}. Tell us the workspace's name and what you saw. If you think someone got into your account, change your password from the sign-in page ("Forgot your password?") and write to us straight away.

We work hard to keep your data safe, but no website is perfectly safe, and we won't promise that nothing will ever go wrong. See also our Terms and Privacy page.
