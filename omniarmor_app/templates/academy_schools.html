It starts with the class's lemonade stand (what a business is, rules that keep customers safe, money and teamwork), then speaking like a pro, owning mistakes, prices, budgets, honest ads, doing the right thing and staying safe online. It ends with the Class Challenge and a certificate. Read the teacher guide: what each world teaches, how long it takes and questions to talk about.

Safe by design

You add students by first name or nickname only. No student email, no birthdate, no photos, and nothing a student types is shown to anyone. Each student gets a drawn picture.

A class progress view

Lessons done for each student, and the class total, on one page. Only you and your school's other staff in OmniArmor can see it.

Certificates to print

A student who finishes the first lesson of every world and passes the Class Challenge earns a certificate of completion with their first name or nickname on it. Print one, or the whole class.

A leaderboard you control

It starts off. Turn it on for a class and its students see their own class's leaderboard: first names and lessons done, nothing else, and never another class. Turn it off again anytime.

Join codes on paper

Print one code per student. Each one works once, on a school device. Students don't need an email or a password.

A trial report for the office

At the end of the free trial, print one page with the totals: how many students, how many lessons finished, and the topics covered. No student names.
