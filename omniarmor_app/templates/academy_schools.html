For schools and teachers

How a business runs, one short lesson at a time.

The Future Owner Academy is a game-like course for kids: money, customers, teamwork, leading, rules and safety, in levels of a few minutes each. Run it for a whole class or an entrepreneurship club. Try it free for {{ facts.pilot_days }} days, no card needed.

Safe by design

You add students by first name or nickname only. No student email, no birthdate, no photos, and nothing a student types is shown to anyone. Each student gets a drawn picture.

A class progress view

Lessons done for each student, and the class total, on one page. Only you and your school's other staff in OmniArmor can see it.

Certificates to print

A student who finishes the first lesson of every world and passes the Owner's Challenge earns a certificate of completion with their first name or nickname on it. Print one, or the whole class.

A leaderboard you control

Students can see their own class's leaderboard: first names and lessons done, nothing else, and never another class. Switch it off anytime.

Join codes on paper

Print one code per student. Each one works once, on a school device. Students don't need an email or a password.

A pilot report for the office

At the end of the pilot, print one page with the totals: how many students, how many lessons finished, and the topics covered. No student names.

How to start

  1. Sign up for free with your school's name.
  2. Open the Academy, then Classes, and start the {{ facts.pilot_days }}-day pilot.
  3. Add your class and type your students' first names or nicknames.
  4. Print the join codes and hand them out. Students play on school devices.
  5. Watch the class progress, print certificates, and take the pilot report to the office.
